# Valor South Austin calendar feeds

Public calendar feeds for Valor South Austin parents. Subscribe in your
calendar app and events update automatically when the schedule changes.

## 2026-27 Student Calendar

**ICS feed URL** (paste this into your calendar app to subscribe):

`https://vsa-cal.github.io/valor-2026-27.ics`

Once subscribed, your calendar app will automatically pull updates
whenever the schedule changes. Pick the instructions for your app below.

### [Subscribe in KashCal](https://kashcal.onekash.org)

1. Open KashCal → Settings → Calendar Feeds (ICS) → Add ICS Calendar
2. Paste the URL above
3. Pick a refresh interval and save

### Subscribe on iPhone

Open the Calendar app or iOS Settings, find "Add Subscribed Calendar"
or "Add Account → Other," and paste the URL above.

### Subscribe in Google Calendar

1. Open Google Calendar on the web
2. Left sidebar: Other calendars → + → From URL
3. Paste the URL above
4. Add calendar

### Subscribe in Outlook

Open Outlook Calendar, look for "Add calendar" or "Subscribe from web,"
and paste the URL above.

### Subscribe in any other calendar app

Look for "Subscribe to URL" or "Add subscription" in your app's settings.
Paste the URL above.

## What's in the feed

Student holidays, early release days, exam days, semester start dates,
parent/teacher conferences, grading-period boundaries, and breaks.
Color-coded to match the school's printed calendar.
